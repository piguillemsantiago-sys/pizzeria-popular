We've arrived in Benidorm!

Our sixth pizzeria opens its doors facing the Mediterranean, in the Balcón del Mediterráneo area.

19 May 2026 Pizzería Popular 5 min read

In this article

Why Benidorm A 9-month build The menu, sea view The team The opening How to visit us
Share WA f X

After 9 months of renovation, we're opening our sixth pizzeria in one of the most beautiful spots on the Costa Blanca, in the Balcón del Mediterráneo area.

¡Hola mi vida! 🔥 Today we want to share something that has us happy, proud and a little emotional: Pizzería Popular is opening its doors in Benidorm, right in the historic old town, just steps from the city's most iconic viewpoint.

It's our sixth pizzeria in Spain, and the first in the northern Costa Blanca. An opening we'd been imagining for months.

Here we don't just serve pizza… we create moments.

— Pizzería Popular

We're open

The new storefront, right on the promenade, a step from the sea.

01 — The placeWhy Benidorm? Because the view won us over

It wasn't a random decision. We chose Benidorm for one simple reason: it has one of the most stunning views in the whole Valencian Community, it sits right in the tourist heart, and the air you breathe there is like nowhere else.

We knew we wanted to bring Pizzería Popular to a place where people come to enjoy, to switch off, to live moments. And Benidorm is exactly that: a city that welcomes millions of visitors every year, but that also has its soul, its lifelong neighbours and its corners full of history.

02 — The storyA 9-month build to bring a sleeping space back to life

The place had been closed for more than 2 years. It was a space that needed a lot of love, a lot of patience and a lot of work. We rolled up our sleeves (and got our hands in the dough) for 9 months to transform it into what it is today: a pizzeria with the Argentine warmth that defines us, with the oven lit and the pasta made to order.

Bringing a space like this back to life isn't just a business project. It's a commitment to the neighbourhood, to the neighbours, and to everyone who stops by to say hello.

03 — The kitchenThe same menu, now with a sea view

At Pizzería Popular Benidorm you'll find the same as in every one of our locations:

  • Artisan pizzas with slow-fermented dough and high-temperature baking.
  • Argentine milanesas that take you straight to Buenos Aires, including the classic napolitana that is our flagship.
  • Fresh pasta, wraps, salads and sandwiches made with carefully selected ingredients.
  • Homemade desserts, from tiramisu to the creamy cheesecake that's requested at every table.

And now, a plus only this location has: a spot in the Balcón del Mediterráneo area, one of the most photographed viewpoints in Spain.

04 — The peopleDiego, leading the new location

As in every Popular, what makes the difference is the team. Diego is our manager in Benidorm and from day one he gave it everything. If you stop by, ask for him. He'll welcome you with the best vibe.

Behind him there's a young, well-trained team with the Popular attitude: treating every guest like family.

05 — The partyThe opening filled our hearts

We threw an opening event we'll never forget. Family, friends, neighbours, guests who've followed us since Russafa, Luceros, Playa San Juan or Boadilla — all together sharing pizza, milanesa, music and hugs.

And the first days already gave us beautiful signs: we opened with 5 stars on Google, with reviews from visitors all over Europe highlighting the quality, the service and, of course, the view. If you missed it, no worries: the celebration is only just beginning.

Steps from the Balcón del Mediterráneo

Benidorm's blue hour, steps from our table.

You're in the absolute centre. If you're visiting Benidorm, we're right on your way:

  • In the Balcón del Mediterráneo area (Mirador del Castell).
  • 2 minutes from the Cañones de Plaza Castelar.
  • 3 minutes from the start of Poniente Beach and the old town.
  • 15 minutes walk (or 5 by car) from Levante Beach.
