🎉 We've arrived in Benidorm! New location facing the Mediterranean

May 19, 2026 · Pizzería Popular

After 9 months of renovation, we're opening our sixth pizzeria in one of the most beautiful spots on the Costa Blanca, just 2 minutes from the Balcón del Mediterráneo.

Hello, dear friend! 🔥

Today we want to share something that has us happy, proud, and a little emotional: Pizzería Popular is opening its doors in Benidorm, right in the heart of the old town, just steps away from the city's most iconic viewpoint.

It's our sixth pizzeria in Spain, and the first on the northern Costa Blanca. An opening we've been dreaming about for months.

Why Benidorm? Because the view stole our hearts

It wasn't a random choice. We picked Benidorm for one simple reason: it has one of the most stunning views in the entire Valencian Community, it sits at the heart of the tourist coast, and the energy you breathe there is unlike anywhere else.

We knew we wanted to bring Pizzería Popular to a place where people come to enjoy, to unwind, to live unforgettable moments. And Benidorm is exactly that: a city that welcomes millions of visitors every year, but that also has its own soul, its lifelong locals, and its corners full of history.

A 9-month renovation to bring a dormant space back to life

The location had been closed for more than 2 years. It was a space that needed a lot of love, patience, and hard work. We rolled up our sleeves (and got our hands in the dough, literally) for 9 months to transform it into what it is today: a pizzeria with the warm Argentine spirit that defines us, with the oven lit and pasta made fresh every day.

Bringing a space like this back to life isn't just a business project. It's a commitment to the neighborhood, to the locals, and to everyone who walks in to say hello.

The same menu, now with sea views

At Pizzería Popular Benidorm you'll find everything we serve at our other locations:

  • Artisan pizzas with slow-fermented dough and high-temperature cooking.
  • Argentine milanesas that transport you straight to Buenos Aires, including our signature napolitana.
  • Fresh pastas, wraps, salads, and sandwiches made with carefully selected ingredients.
  • Homemade desserts, from tiramisu to our famously creamy cheesecake.

And now, a bonus only this location offers: a spot just 2 minutes from the Balcón del Mediterráneo, one of Spain's most photographed viewpoints.

Diego, leading our new location

As in every Popular, what truly makes the difference is the team. Diego is our manager in Benidorm, and from day one he's been all in. If you stop by, ask for him. He'll welcome you with the best vibe.

Behind him is a young, well-trained team carrying the Popular attitude: treating every guest like family.

The opening filled our souls

We hosted a grand opening we won't forget. Family, friends, neighbors, and customers who already follow us from Russafa, Luceros, Playa San Juan, or Boadilla — all together sharing pizza, milanesa, music, and hugs.

And the first days have already given us beautiful signs: we opened with 5 stars on Google, with reviews from visitors all over Europe highlighting the quality, the service, and, of course, the view. If you missed it, don't worry — the celebration is just getting started.

Where to find us

📍 Pizzería Popular Benidorm (search for us on Google Maps)

Address: C. del Condestable Zaragoza, 42 — 03501 Benidorm, Alicante

📞 Reservations: +34 680 22 34 58

🕘 Opening hours: Every day from 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM (continuous kitchen, no midday closures).

How to get here from main attractions

  • 2 minutes walking from the Balcón del Mediterráneo (Mirador del Castell).
  • 2 minutes from the Cañones de Plaza Castelar.
  • 3 minutes from the start of Playa de Poniente and the old town.
  • 15 minutes walking (or 5 by car) from Playa de Levante.

You're in the absolute center. If you're visiting Benidorm, you'll walk right past us.
